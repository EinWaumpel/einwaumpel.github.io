"The most important research is the research nobody asked for."

We founded the Institute for Completely Unnecessary Research in 1987, following a dispute about whether hot dogs are sandwiches. That argument remains unresolved. We have since spent 37 years and considerable funding studying things that are fascinating to exactly three people, one of whom is our landlord.

Our peer review process involves sending papers to anyone who replies to our emails. Our ethics board meets quarterly, and has not yet formed a quorum. We are accredited by the Association of Self-Accrediting Institutions (which we also founded).

We are proud to say that not a single one of our findings has been used to make anything worse. This is, we believe, our greatest contribution to humanity.

Prof. R. Absurdington, Founder & Chief Wonderer
