Continuous instant compute: switch this on and the app keeps recalculating the entire visible path mathematically, every frame, as a advances — rather than animating by appending one new point at a time. Switch it off to go back to plain point-by-point animation.

Show end image instantly: switch this on to skip animation entirely — enter a target a and the app calculates the whole path mathematically and draws only the finished picture, with nothing animating through the intermediate values. Editing the target while this is on redraws instantly. Switch it off to resume animating from where it left off.

Symmetry points: click “+ Add point” to draw the picture with multiple points at once instead of one. They're spaced with perfectly equal angular spacing around the origin — e.g. with 2 points, the second is always the exact negative (mirror through the center) of the first; with 4 points, they sit 90° apart; and so on. Each point gets its own editable color.

The canvas keeps the recent path of the moving point. Because both sine/cosine factors are periodic, changing b and c changes the frequencies and symmetry of the picture. Increase the trail length to reveal more of the long-term pattern.
